- ~~**What**: Engine bug (upstream, ironhold-lib) — flycam never renders GLB Prop meshes~~ **RESOLVED — this was never an engine bug.** Root cause: `rotation_euler_deg: (0,0,0)` on a `"flycam"`-tagged entity faces **-Z** (standard Bevy identity-rotation convention), not +Z. `showcase.scene.ron`'s flycam was authored at `translation: (0,5,-8)` with content placed at `z: 0..157` — i.e. content on the camera's `+Z` side — so at yaw 0 the camera was quietly facing straight away from everything. Ground/primitives still rendered (they're what the camera actually saw, an empty stretch behind the content), which made it look exactly like "GLB Props never render" — pitch changes, moving closer, waiting longer, and even updating the engine (see below) all changed nothing, because none of those fix a camera facing the wrong way. Caught by testing a top-down shot (`rotation_euler_deg: (-90,0,0)`) per Frank's suggestion — pitch -90 renders correctly regardless of yaw, which is what finally separated "wrong direction" from "doesn't render." Confirmed by testing yaw 180 at the exact position/distance that had shown nothing: props appeared immediately. **Fix**: `rotation_euler_deg: (0, 180, 0)` on `showcase.scene.ron`'s flycam — restores full free-fly exploration, no workaround needed. `ironhold-lib`'s own `custom_materials` demo places its flycam on the correct (+Z-facing-content) side for exactly this reason, which is why it never hit this. Noted as a durable gotcha in `CLAUDE.md`'s debugging tips instead. `start_menu.scene.ron` keeps its `menu_camera` (player-tag + `camera_mode: Fixed`) — not as a bug workaround, but as a deliberate choice: a menu backdrop should be a locked, composed shot, not something WASD/mouse-look can wander away from.
- **Why kept as an entry**: The misdiagnosis (and the two-commit trail it produced) is worth leaving visible so a future session doesn't re-discover "flycam doesn't render GLB Props," waste time re-filing it upstream, or re-apply the Fixed-camera workaround to a scene that doesn't need it.
- **While**: Implementing the start menu (`start_menu.scene.ron`) and its decorative GLB backdrop — the backdrop rendered as an empty gradient, misread as an engine bug, "fixed" with a Fixed-camera workaround, "confirmed" against an engine update that didn't fix it (because there was nothing to fix), then correctly root-caused after Frank asked to double-check camera orientation from multiple perspectives including top-down.
