# The Integration Program

## Official Name

**Meridian Accord Biological Integration Program**

Nobody calls it that.

- **Internally**: the Integration Program, or just *Integration*
- **The soldiers**: *Integrates* (formal), or simply *the team*
- **The public and press**: *the Accord Operatives* or *the Accord's people*
- **The Compact**: *the Accord's paramilitary asset* (not said to their faces)

---

## What It Is

The program's founding principle, written by Petra Solander when she finally lost the vote against it:

> *Not soldiers, but people who can go where others cannot.*

The Integration Program was created because the Accord kept encountering situations that required more than standard personnel could handle — extreme environments, territorial alien fauna, fringe conditions that standard equipment and training couldn't meet. The biological enhancement program was the answer. The soldier training was a consequence of that answer, not its purpose.

This distinction matters enormously to the people who run the program. It matters less and less to the people who use it.

---

## The Biological Enhancement

The enhancements are internal. Glandular, neurological, and immune modifications that allow soldiers to function in conditions that would incapacitate or kill unenhanced personnel. The full suite takes approximately eighteen months to integrate properly and several more years of conditioning to stabilize.

Key enhancements across all Integrates:
- Accelerated environmental adaptation (temperature range, atmospheric pressure tolerance, radiation thresholds)
- Enhanced immune response to novel biological threats
- Improved cardiovascular and musculoskeletal performance
- Neurological adaptation for faster sensory processing under stress

The enhancements are cumulative and permanent. There is no reversal process. Soldiers who leave the program carry their modifications for life. The Accord provides ongoing medical support for all former Integrates.

---

## The Enhancement Suites

The biological enhancements cover the range of conditions most Reach operations involve. For environments beyond that range, external suits extend the biological platform further.

| Name | Called | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Core Integration** | "the Kit" / "Core" | Standard environments, limited vacuum, most field work |
| **Void Integration** | "Void Kit" | Extended space and near-vacuum operations |
| **Thermal Integration** | "Thermal" | Extreme heat — volcanic, industrial, stellar proximity |
| **Cryo Integration** | "Cryo" | Extreme cold — deep space surface, cryo-world operations |
| **Hazard Integration** | "Haz" | Acidic, toxic, and high-radiation environments |

Soldiers spend most missions in Core. Pulling a Void or Haz kit means the mission just became serious. There is a superstition on Vantis that checking out a Thermal kit before reading the briefing means you have made a mistake somewhere.

Suit assignment is based on mission parameters and medical compatibility. Not every Integrate is cleared for every suit — individual biology matters. Mira's unusual enhancement profile means she has broader compatibility than most.

## The Program's Track Record

The Integration Program is the most successful enhanced soldier program in the Reach.

This is not a marketing claim. It is a documented outcome: lowest rate of enhancement-related medical complications, lowest friendly-casualty rate across comparable operations, highest mission success rate in fringe environments. The program works because it treats its soldiers as people with biology to be supported rather than assets to be optimized.

This track record is the program's greatest strength and, via the Competence Snare, its greatest vulnerability.

## Other Programs

Several factions and corporations have attempted their own enhancement programs. Most have failed to some degree.

### Why They Fail

The common failures:
- **Accelerated integration**: Pushing the timeline to produce soldiers faster than the biology can adapt. Results in cascading systemic failures, some immediately apparent, some delayed.
- **Optimization over sustainability**: Designing for peak performance rather than stable long-term function. Early results look excellent. Later results do not.
- **Inadequate support infrastructure**: Enhancement is not a one-time procedure. It requires ongoing medical monitoring, environmental calibration, and individual adjustment. Programs that treat it as a deployment pipeline rather than a medical relationship produce compromised soldiers.

### The Programs That Went Further

Some programs, typically those with more resources and less oversight, attempted to incorporate non-human biological material — alien DNA, alien-derived compounds, biological structures from territorial fauna — into the enhancement process.

The results were difficult to control.

The creatures the team encounters on certain cleanup operations — aggressive, wrong-looking, not entirely animal — were people once. The modification attempted something the biology could not accommodate. The Accord is occasionally sent to handle what remains.

**Who funded some of those programs is a question with an uncomfortable answer.**

## Mira's Program

Mira Osei came from one of these failed programs. Her program did not incorporate alien biology — it simply pushed too hard, too fast. When enough soldiers developed unmanageable side effects, the program could no longer be hidden. It collapsed.

The Accord stepped in. Petra authorized Mira's recruitment when the medical board hesitated. Mira was stabilized — not fixed, because fully fixing her was not possible — and integrated into the standard program.

What remains:
- **Faster environmental adaptation** than standard Integrates — her modifications settled into an unusual configuration that produces accelerated baseline adaptation as a side effect.
- **Sensory episodes** — under extreme stress or certain alien biochemical exposures, she experiences heightened perception that becomes overload. Managed, not dangerous, but present.

Mira knows her previous program had funding that was not fully disclosed. She has not yet determined whose.

## What the Program Is Becoming

The founding principle was *not soldiers, but people who can go where others cannot.*

The missions are increasingly indistinguishable from military operations. Faction elimination. Rogue operative cleanup. The work the Compact would otherwise do, handled by the Accord because the Accord does it with fewer casualties and better outcomes.

Every mission done well makes the next request easier to justify. Every request accepted makes the program look more like a military asset. Every confirmation that it looks like a military asset gives Voss stronger grounds to argue for military jurisdiction.

The team knows this. They keep doing the work anyway, because the work in front of them is real and the people who need help are real and the political consequences are abstract in a way that a person in a bad situation is not.

This is both their best quality and the mechanism of the trap.
